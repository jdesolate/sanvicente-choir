# CHANGES PROPOSED — For Officer Decision
## San Vicente Choir | Prepared June 2026

These items touch the substance of ratified rules or depend on observed member
behavior. They require officer deliberation — not a unilateral edit. Each entry
includes the location, the concern, and a suggested option.

---

### 1. Mass Quorum — Article V, Section 4

**Current wording:**
> "A minimum of 4 members, with at least 1 per voice part, is required to
> proceed with a mass."

**Concern:**
With current group numbers (sometimes only 3 present, and sections frequently
unbalanced), the strict "1 per voice part" requirement will regularly fail. The
choir may be unable to proceed even when enough people are present to give
a dignified service.

**Suggested option:**
Replace "with at least 1 per voice part" with "balanced across voice parts as
far as possible." This preserves the musical intent (aim for balance) without
making a quorum mechanically impossible on days when one section has no
representative.

**Officers must decide:** Keep strict 1-per-part, or soften to "as far as
possible"?

---

### 2. Weekly Poll Dependency — Article V, Section 2

**Current wording:**
> "A poll for the coming Saturday and Sunday masses shall be posted in the
> group chat every Thursday before 9PM. All Active Members are expected to
> respond to the poll."

**Concern:**
This system works only if members actively engage with the group chat. Current
group-chat engagement is low. A poll that goes unread or ignored defeats its
planning purpose.

**This is not a wording fix.** The rule is sound. The risk is behavioral:
the poll needs to be paired with personal follow-up by section leaders on
members who haven't responded by Thursday evening. Without that human layer,
the poll becomes a formality rather than a planning tool.

**Suggested option:**
Add a note in the officer operating procedures (not in the Constitution) that
section leaders do a brief personal check-in with their members on Thursdays
if poll responses are incomplete. No constitutional amendment needed — just an
operational commitment.

**Officers must decide:** How will section leaders follow up on unanswered
polls in practice?

---

### 3. Monthly Meeting Load — Article XII, Section 2

**Current wording:**
> "At least one (1) mandatory general meeting per month shall be held for all
> Active Members."

**Concern:**
A mandatory monthly meeting may be difficult to sustain with a small, busy
group in the founding year. If meetings feel like a burden rather than a
gathering, attendance will drop — which creates a quorum problem (quorum is
5 Active Members) and could create a cycle of low-energy meetings.

**Suggested option A:** Keep mandatory monthly meetings but allow them to be
held immediately before or after a Saturday mass (reducing the burden of
scheduling a separate trip).

**Suggested option B:** For the founding year only (2026–2027), formally soften
"mandatory" to "strongly encouraged" — restoring the mandatory standard in
Year 2 when the group is more stable. This would require a transitional note
(similar to Article XV-A) or a 2/3 vote to amend.

**Officers must decide:** Keep mandatory as-is, or soften for the founding year?

---

### 4. Unfilled Officer and Section-Leader Roles — Article IV, Section 1

**Current situation:**
Article IV, Section 1 already states that "not all positions need to be filled
every term." However, the Constitution does not record which positions are
intentionally left vacant for 2026–2027.

**Concern:**
Without a formal record of which roles are vacant and by intent (not by
oversight), it may be unclear whether the choir is functioning as planned or
whether gaps exist that need urgent attention. It also risks a few members
carrying the load of multiple roles.

**Suggested option:**
At the June 20, 2026 General Assembly, formally record (in the minutes) the
exact list of positions intentionally left vacant for the 2026–2027 founding
term. This list serves as the baseline for Year 2 — when the choir can
deliberately decide which roles to fill as the group grows.

Positions to consider explicitly:
- Treasurer (currently VACANT — most urgent to fill)
- Soprano, Alto, Tenor, Bass Section Leaders
- Music Committee Head
- Membership Committee Head
- Logistics & Property Head
- Documentation & Social Media Head

**Officers must decide:** Which of the above are intentionally vacant vs.
actively needed to fill by mid-term?
