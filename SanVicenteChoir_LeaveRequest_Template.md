> **HOW TO USE THIS TEMPLATE:**
> 1. Fill in all fields marked with [ ] or blank lines
> 2. Print on clean paper — a printed letter on clean stationery looks more official
> 3. Must be signed by the President or any officer
> 4. Request must be made by the member **at least 5 days before** the activity when possible, to give their employer enough time to process
> 5. This letter is a **support document** — it does not guarantee approval from the employer. The final decision rests with the company.
> 6. Keep a copy of all issued letters for choir records
> 7. For **Major Events** (Holy Week, Christmas, Sinulog) — encourage working members to request early, ideally 2–3 weeks in advance
