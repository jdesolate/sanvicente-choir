# CONSTITUTION AND BY-LAWS
## San Vicente Choir
### Established in Service Since 1972 | Formalized June 20, 2026

---

## PREAMBLE

We, the members of San Vicente Choir, united in faith and music for over 54 years of continuous service to God and the community of Archdiocesan Shrine of San Nicolas de Tolentino Parish, do hereby establish this Constitution and By-Laws as the foundation of our shared commitment.

We serve willingly — not by force, not by guilt, but by love for God and for each other.

We believe that every member who has offered their voice, however briefly, has contributed to something greater than themselves. We believe that those who serve consistently deserve support and clarity. We believe that those who must step away deserve dignity. And we believe that those who return are always welcome.

This Constitution exists not to punish, but to protect — to give every generation of San Vicente Choir a clear foundation so that the confusion of the past need never repeat itself, and so that every member, from the newest voice to the most veteran, knows exactly where they stand.

*"Sing to the Lord a new song; sing to the Lord, all the earth."* — Psalm 96:1

---

## ARTICLE I — NAME AND IDENTITY

**Section 1.** The organization shall be known as **San Vicente Choir**, hereafter referred to as "the Choir."

**Section 2.** The Choir is a voluntary, faith-based music ministry primarily serving the liturgical needs of San Vicente Ferrer Chapel, Duljo Brgy. Hall (Saturday 7PM Mass) and Archdiocesan Shrine of San Nicolas de Tolentino Parish, Cebu City (Sunday 7PM Mass).

**Section 3.** The Choir is not a paid ensemble. Members serve as an act of worship and community. Any honoraria or funds received are communal resources managed transparently by the officers.

**Section 4.** This Constitution supersedes all previous informal agreements, rules, or understandings as of June 20, 2026, the date of its ratification.

---

## ARTICLE II — VISION AND MISSION

**Vision:** A choir where every member grows — in confidence, in faith, in skill, in friendship — and can one day say: *"I served God with everything I had, and I am better for it."*

**Mission:** To provide joyful, prepared, and reverent music ministry in service of the parish liturgy, to welcome new members with warmth, to support each other through life's seasons, and to honor the 54-year legacy of those who sang before us.

## ARTICLE III — MEMBERSHIP

**Section 1. Eligibility.**

San Vicente Choir welcomes anyone who wishes to serve God through music. The following criteria apply:

**(a) Singing Members**
- Must be at least in **Junior High School (Grade 7, approximately 13 years old)**
- No maximum age — service to God has no expiration
- No prior musical experience or training required — a willingness to learn and to serve is sufficient
- Must agree to uphold the values, conduct, and attendance expectations in this Constitution
- Must sign the Membership Form upon joining and at every annual renewal

**(b) Ministry Support Members**
Instrumentalists and accompanists (e.g., guitarist, keyboardist, bassist) who serve the Choir in a supporting musical capacity. Ministry Support Members:
- Have no age restriction
- Must be evaluated by the Music Director as ready to accompany the Choir in a liturgical setting
- Are subject to the same Code of Conduct, attire, and mass behavior standards as all members
- Hold voting rights if also registered as Active Singing Members; otherwise, they are non-voting participants unless the officers decide otherwise by majority vote

**(c) Members Below 18 Years Old (Minors)**
- A parent or guardian must co-sign the Membership Form before the minor may participate in any choir activity
- The Choir shall exercise appropriate care and discretion in all activities involving minor members
- Officers may communicate with parents or guardians regarding a minor's welfare, conduct, or participation as necessary

**Section 2. Membership Categories.**

**(a) Active Member**
A member who has signed the Membership Form, committed to the attendance expectations in Article V, and participates in the weekly attendance poll. Active Members hold voting rights in general assemblies.

**(b) Associate Member (On Leave)**
A member who, due to personal circumstances (pregnancy, board review, work schedule, health, family), cannot fulfill weekly attendance but wishes to remain part of the Choir community. Associate Members:
- Are not subject to attendance penalties
- Remain in the group chat
- May participate in masses when available and willing
- Do not hold voting rights while on Associate status
- May return to Active status at any time by notifying an officer

**(c) Honorary Member**
A member who has served the Choir for a significant number of years and whose circumstances no longer allow regular participation. Honorary Members are recognized at the annual assembly and are always welcome to sing.

**Section 3. Joining Process.**
New members must complete the following steps in order:

1. **Express interest** to any officer — in person, through the group chat, or through any available digital platform the Choir maintains
2. **Fill out the Registration Form** — providing basic personal information before proceeding further
3. **Receive and read the Member Handbook and this Constitution** — the applicant must be given time to read and understand both documents fully before signing anything
4. **Sign the Membership Form** — acknowledging they have read, understood, and agreed to the Constitution and Handbook. For minors, a parent or guardian must co-sign.
5. **Audition** — the applicant sings at least one (1) song of their choice before available choir members and officers. The purpose of the audition is:
   - To assess and assign the appropriate voice section (Soprano, Alto, Tenor, or Bass)
   - To allow the choir to welcome and affirm the applicant as a new member
   - The audition is **not a rejection test** — it is a welcome tradition. Applicants who audition in good faith are accepted into the choir.
6. **Voice section assignment** — the Music Director, in consultation with available Section Leaders, assigns the new member to their appropriate voice section based on the audition
7. **Introduction to the choir** — the new member is formally introduced to the full choir at the next available mass or meeting

*Note: The Choir envisions transitioning this process to a digital platform in the future — including online registration forms, digital signing of the Constitution and Handbook, and section assignment tracking — to reduce paperwork and improve accessibility for all members.*

**Section 4. Annual Renewal.**
Every June, all Active Members will sign a renewal form. This is not a test of loyalty — it is an annual moment for each member to personally renew their commitment and state their personal goal for the year.

**Section 5. Voluntary Departure.**
Any member who wishes to leave the Choir permanently shall:
1. Notify any officer personally (in person, call, or private message)
2. Be given a proper farewell acknowledgment in the group chat or at the next meeting
3. Never be kicked from the group chat without their consent

No member shall be forcibly removed from the group chat or the Choir without the formal process described in Article XI.

## ARTICLE IV — OFFICERS

**Section 1. Officer Positions.**

The Choir recognizes the following officer positions. Not all positions need to be filled every term — the officers shall decide annually which roles are active based on available members and current needs.

**Executive & Advisory**

| Position | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **President** | Overall leadership, final decisions, represents the Choir to the parish, leads meetings, mediates conflicts |
| **Vice President & Internal Coordinator** | Supports the President, acts as President in absence, oversees member welfare, morale, and internal communications |
| **Choir Adviser** | Senior advisory role, provides institutional wisdom, mentors officers, does not hold executive authority but must be consulted on major decisions |

**Musical Leadership**

| Position | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Music Director & Liturgical Lead** | Leads all musical preparation, coordinates song selection with parish liturgical guidelines, trains members, manages the Master Repertoire List |
| **Soprano Section Leader** | Leads and supports soprano members in preparation and attendance |
| **Alto Section Leader** | Leads and supports alto members in preparation and attendance |
| **Tenor Section Leader** | Leads and supports tenor members in preparation and attendance |
| **Bass Section Leader** | Leads and supports bass members in preparation and attendance |

**Administrative Officers**

| Position | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Secretary** | Records minutes, maintains membership records, manages Membership Forms, handles announcements, documents Choir history |
| **External Coordinator** | Liaises with parishes (San Nicolas, SVF Chapel), venues, sponsors, and external groups; handles scheduling with parish coordinators. May be combined with Secretary role or assigned separately. |
| **Treasurer** | Manages all funds, records all income and expenses, presents financial report to all members at every General Assembly |

**Committee Heads**
Committees are formed to engage all members — new and old — and distribute the workload across the group. Each committee is led by a Head appointed or elected by officers. Committee structure is reviewed annually and may be adjusted without amending this Constitution.

| Committee | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Music Committee** | Song research, rehearsal coordination, repertoire suggestions |
| **Membership Committee** | New member onboarding, tracking member status, follow-up on inactive members |
| **Logistics & Property** | Sound system, sheet music folders, equipment care, event setup |
| **Documentation & Social Media** | Photos, videos, social media posts, media archiving |
| **Costume Committee** | *(Optional — activated when needed for performances or competitions)* |

**Section 2. Election.**
- Officers are elected annually at the **May Anniversary Assembly** (2nd or 3rd weekend of May) by a majority vote of Active Members present.
- Oath-taking of newly elected officers shall follow within the last week of May.
- The outgoing officers shall complete their handover to incoming officers before the June General Assembly.
- Any Active Member in good standing may nominate themselves or be nominated by another member.
- Nominees must accept or decline the nomination before voting proceeds.
- No one may be assigned an officer role without their consent.

**Section 3. Term.**
Officers serve a term of one (1) year — from oath-taking in May until the next oath-taking in May of the following year. Terms are renewable with no limit on the number of consecutive terms.

**Section 4. Resignation.**
Any officer may resign at any time by notifying the President (or Vice President if the President is resigning) in writing. The remaining officers shall appoint a replacement by majority vote until the next election.

Before a resigning officer is considered fully released from their role, they are expected to complete a **handover** to their replacement, which includes:
- Explaining the responsibilities of the position in person or in writing
- Turning over any documents, records, funds, or materials related to the role
- Being available for questions from the replacement for a reasonable transition period

This handover is an act of respect for the Choir and for the person taking over. No officer who resigns in good standing shall be denied a proper farewell.

**Section 5. Succession.**
- If the President is unable to serve, the Vice President assumes the role.
- If any officer is unable to continue mid-term, the remaining officers shall appoint a replacement by majority vote until the next election.
- If the Music Lead/Choir Director is temporarily or permanently unable to serve, the Choir Adviser and the most senior available instrumentalist shall jointly assume music leadership until a new Music Lead is designated by officer vote.

**Section 6. Officer Code of Conduct.**
All officers commit to:
- No public call-outs, threats, or shaming in the group chat or any public setting
- Addressing concerns with members privately and respectfully first
- Representing all members fairly, regardless of seniority or attendance record
- Maintaining confidentiality of personal matters shared by members
- Upholding the founding principle: members serve willingly, not by guilt

Violation of this Code of Conduct by an officer may result in a formal review by the remaining officers and, if warranted, removal from the officer position by majority officer vote.

## ARTICLE V — ATTENDANCE

**Section 1. Event Tiers.**

| Tier | Events | Expectation |
|---|---|---|
| **Core** | Saturday 7PM Mass (SVF Chapel), Sunday 7PM Mass (San Nicolas) | Expected of all Active Members |
| **Major** | Christmas, Holy Week, Patron Saint Feast, other significant parish liturgies | Required of all Active Members unless excused |
| **Special** | Competitions, concerts, gigs, seminars | Voluntary; announced in advance |

**Section 2. Weekly Attendance Poll.**
- A poll for the coming Saturday and Sunday masses shall be posted in the group chat every **Thursday before 9PM**.
- All Active Members are expected to respond to the poll.
- No response by Friday 12NN = counted as absent for planning purposes.
- Responding "No" is not a penalty — it is responsible communication.

**Section 3. Absence Classification.**

| Type | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Excused — No Notice Needed** | Work shift, school exam/requirement, medical emergency, family emergency |
| **Excused — Prior Notice Required** | School organization, out of town, personal reason — must inform an officer before the mass |
| **Unexcused** | No poll response, no notice, no reason given |

**Section 4. Quorum for Mass.**
A minimum of **4 members, with at least 1 per voice part**, is required to proceed with a mass. If quorum is not met, the President or designated officer shall decide whether to proceed or notify the parish coordinator. This decision is the officer's — no single member bears that burden alone.

*Note: Mass quorum is a musical and liturgical standard — it ensures the choir can serve the congregation with dignity. It is separate from meeting quorum, which governs organizational decisions.*

**Section 5. Attendance Review.**
If an Active Member accumulates **3 consecutive unexcused absences**, an officer will reach out privately to check on them — not to scold, but to understand. Based on that conversation, the member may:
- Return to regular participation
- Shift to Associate Member status voluntarily
- Or the officer may recommend a status change with the member's knowledge

No status change shall happen without the member being informed directly and personally.

## ARTICLE VI — REHEARSALS AND PRACTICES

**Section 1. Definitions.**

*(a) Personal Practice* — Individual preparation done by a member on their own time, such as listening to assigned songs, studying lyrics, or following voice part recordings at home. Members are encouraged to personally practice before group rehearsals, especially for new or unfamiliar songs.

*(b) Rehearsal* — An official group session where members sing together, with or without instrumentalists and/or the Choir Director present. Rehearsals are scheduled by the officers and constitute an official choir activity subject to attendance expectations.

**Section 2. Regular Rehearsal Schedule.**
- At minimum, **one (1) official rehearsal per month** shall be held throughout the year
- The schedule for each semester shall be set by the officers through a **member availability poll** posted in the group chat at least **2 weeks before the semester begins**
- During months leading up to **Major Events** (Christmas season, Holy Week, competitions), rehearsals shall increase to **one to two (1–2) sessions per week** depending on group availability

**Section 3. Rehearsal Time Standards.**
All official rehearsals follow this schedule:

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 7:30 PM | Call time / Meet-up — members are expected to arrive |
| 7:45 PM | Warm-up begins |
| 8:00 PM | Rehearsal officially starts |

- Rehearsal shall begin at **8:00 PM sharp** regardless of how many members have arrived, provided quorum is met
- **Quorum for rehearsal:** At least **4 Active Members** must be present for rehearsal to proceed
- The **Choir Director** oversees all rehearsals. In his absence, the Music Committee Head or the most senior available member shall lead
- A **song plan or rehearsal agenda** must be prepared by the Choir Director or Music Committee Head and shared with members **before the rehearsal day**

**Section 4. Rehearsal Cancellation.**
Rehearsal may be cancelled due to:
- Heavy rain or typhoon signal
- Emergency situations affecting the majority of members
- Force majeure or parish-declared suspension of activities

Cancellation must be announced in the group chat by an officer **as early as possible**, and the rehearsal shall be rescheduled at the next available date.

**Section 5. Rehearsal Attendance.**
Active Members are expected to attend all scheduled rehearsals. Absence from rehearsal follows the same excused/unexcused classification in Article V, Section 3. Consistent rehearsal attendance directly affects the choir's readiness and quality of service.

**Section 6. Rest Period After Major Events.**
After every Major Event (Holy Week, Christmas season, major competitions or performances), the Choir shall observe a mandatory **minimum rest period of one (1) week** during which no rehearsals or non-liturgical activities shall be scheduled.

- Regular **Saturday and Sunday mass obligations continue** during the rest period — the rest applies to rehearsals and additional activities only
- Officers may extend the rest period beyond one week at their discretion
- The rest period is a recognition that consistent members deserve recovery time and that the Choir's long-term health depends on sustainable service

**Section 7. Excuse Letters for Student Members.**
The Choir recognizes that student members may require official documentation to excuse absences from school due to choir activities, performances, or events. Upon request:

- Any officer, preferably the **President or Secretary**, may issue a signed Excuse Letter on behalf of San Vicente Choir
- The letter shall state the nature of the choir activity, the date(s), and the member's participation
- Requests for excuse letters must be made **at least 3 days before** the activity when possible
- The Choir shall maintain a standard Excuse Letter template for this purpose

**Section 8. Leave Request Letters for Working Members.**
The Choir recognizes that working members may need official documentation to support a leave request from their employer in order to participate in choir activities or major events. Upon request:

- Any officer, preferably the **President or Secretary**, may issue a signed Leave Request Letter on behalf of San Vicente Choir addressed to the member's employer
- The letter shall state the nature of the choir activity, the date(s), and the member's role in the Choir
- Requests must be made **at least 5 days before** the activity when possible, to allow the member sufficient time to process the request with their employer
- For **Major Events** (Holy Week, Christmas season, Sinulog), working members are encouraged to request the letter **2–3 weeks in advance**
- The Choir shall maintain a standard Leave Request Letter template for this purpose
- This letter serves as a support document — the Choir acknowledges that final approval rests with the employer and does not guarantee leave approval

## ARTICLE VII — CODE OF CONDUCT, ATTIRE, AND MASS DISCIPLINE

**Section 1. Core Values.**
Every member of San Vicente Choir commits to upholding the following values in all choir activities:
- **Faith First** — We sing for God before we sing for anyone else
- **Respect** — For each other, for the clergy, for the congregation, and for ourselves
- **Responsibility** — Show up, communicate, and follow through on commitments
- **Growth** — Every member is here to grow musically, personally, and spiritually
- **Honesty** — Raise concerns properly through officers, not through gossip or public shaming
- **Inclusion** — New members are welcomed, returning members are celebrated, departing members are honored
- **Excellence** — Not perfection, but always giving our best

**Section 2. Assume Positive Intention.**
Our community brings together people with different schedules, personalities, and circumstances. Before reacting to a missed mass, an unanswered poll, or a comment that didn't land well, members are expected to pause and ask first: *"Is there a good reason I don't know yet?"*

Practical guidelines:
- **Before assuming the worst — ask.** A simple *"Okay ka ba?"* goes further than a complaint.
- **Tone is hard to read in text.** If a message seems curt or cold, assume it was written in a hurry — not in anger.
- **Absence is not rejection.** When someone doesn't show up or doesn't reply, they probably have a reason. Check on them; don't judge them.
- **Disagreement is not a personal attack.** When someone questions a decision, assume they care about the group — not that they are opposing you personally.
- **Speak to the person, not about them.** If something bothers you, raise it directly with the person or with an officer — not in a side conversation or the group chat.
- **Give grace.** Everyone has hard days at school, at work, at home. What looks like a bad attitude is sometimes just exhaustion.

*The goal is not blind positivity — it is the discipline to pause before judging. This is how a choir becomes a community.*

**Section 3. Proper Attire.**
How we dress reflects the dignity of our ministry. We are visible to the priest, lay ministers, and the entire congregation.

*(a) During Practices and Rehearsals:*
- At minimum, a T-shirt with sleeves must be worn
- Sleeveless shirts and revealing clothing are not permitted

*(b) During Masses and Liturgical Services:*
- At minimum, a neat and clean T-shirt or collared shirt, paired with pants or jeans
- Shorts, sleeveless clothing, very short skirts, or overly casual and revealing clothing are not permitted

Members who arrive for a mass not meeting the dress standard may be asked by an officer to step back until they can change, at the officer's discretion.

**Section 4. Behavior During Mass.**
When serving during a mass, members are in a visible and sacred role. The following standards apply:

- **Talking must be kept to a minimum.** If communication is necessary for music coordination, it must be done in a whisper or very low voice.
- **Laughing, giggling, or unnecessary side conversations** while singing or standing at the microphone are not permitted. Joy is expressed through song — not through distracting behavior.
- **Phone use** during the mass is limited to music sheet reference only. Social media and personal messaging are not permitted while the mass is ongoing.
- **Attentiveness is expected.** Members must watch the Music Director for cues and be aware of when to stand, sit, and sing.
- **Every song should be sung with intention.** Even with an unfamiliar song, members are expected to try.

We are a direct representation of Archdiocesan Shrine of San Nicolas de Tolentino Parish during every service. Our behavior — not just our singing — reflects on the entire choir community.

**Section 5. Group Chat Conduct.**
- No public call-outs, threats, or shaming of any member in the group chat
- Concerns about attendance or behavior are to be raised privately with an officer first
- Announcements and coordination are the primary purpose of the group chat
- All members, including officers, are expected to maintain a respectful and encouraging tone

## ARTICLE VIII — FINANCES

**Section 1. Nature of Funds.**
San Vicente Choir is a voluntary, faith-based music ministry. The Choir does not require membership dues and does not depend on any single source of income to operate. Any funds received — whether from parish honoraria, talent fees, donations, sponsorships, or other sources — are supplemental to the Choir's mission and are treated as communal resources managed transparently by the officers.

The Choir's ability to serve God and the parish does not depend on money. Funds exist to support the group, not define it.

**Section 2. Sources of Funds.**
The Choir may receive funds from:
- Parish honoraria, if provided by the parish at their discretion
- Talent fees from performances or singing engagements
- Donations from benefactors, alumni, or community supporters
- Sponsorships solicited for specific events, uniforms, or equipment
- Other voluntary contributions

**Section 3. Use of Funds.**
Funds shall be used for purposes that benefit the Choir and its members, including but not limited to:
- Transportation for choir events and performances
- Group uniforms or choir attire
- Equipment, sound system maintenance, or music materials
- Seminars, workshops, or music ministry formation
- Group events and fellowship activities
- **Community assistance** — the Choir may allocate funds to help a current or former member in need (medical, emergency, bereavement), subject to officer approval by majority vote

**Section 4. Financial Management.**
- The Treasurer shall maintain a written record of all income and expenses
- A financial report shall be presented to all members at every General Assembly, or upon request by any Active Member
- Any expenditure above **₱300** must be approved by a majority of officers
- Emergency expenditures below ₱300 may be approved by the President and Treasurer jointly, with full disclosure at the next meeting

**Section 5. Transparency.**
All members have the right to know how choir funds are managed. The Treasurer's records are open to any member upon request. No funds shall be used for personal benefit of any officer or member.

**Section 6. End-of-Term Turnover.**
At the end of each term, the outgoing Treasurer shall present a full financial report and turn over all funds, records, and receipts to the incoming Treasurer, with the President and one other officer as witnesses.

## ARTICLE IX — COMMUNITY PROGRAMS AND DEVELOPMENT

**Section 1. Purpose.**
Beyond liturgical service, San Vicente Choir is committed to the growth, wellness, and community impact of its members. The Choir shall organize regular activities that strengthen fellowship, develop individual skills, and serve the wider community.

**Section 2. Saturday Community Program.**
On Saturdays when no major parish event is scheduled, the Choir is encouraged to organize a **Saturday Group Activity** to strengthen fellowship, develop skills, and increase participation at the Saturday 7PM mass. The timing is flexible based on what the group decides:

- **Before mass (3:00 PM – 5:00 PM)** — recreational activities, seminars, community outreach, or fellowship
- **After mass (8:00 PM onwards)** — optional practice session, casual bonding, or group discussion

Activities shall rotate based on member interest and officer planning, and may include but are not limited to:

| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| 🏃 **Recreational / Wellness** | Jogging, exercise, badminton, pickleball, sports |
| 🎵 **Practice Session** | Group rehearsal, sectional practice, or music review after mass |
| 📚 **Seminar / Workshop** | Vocal training, music ministry workshops, faith formation, invited speakers |
| 🤝 **Community Service** | Teaching young children to sing, parish outreach, volunteer service |
| 🎉 **Fellowship / Fun** | Casual bonding, celebrations, team-building |

- Saturday activities are **optional but encouraged** — not subject to attendance penalties
- Schedule and timing must be announced at least **3 days in advance**
- Officers plan the activity rotation each semester through a group poll

**Section 3. Community Outreach.**
The Choir is encouraged to share its gifts beyond the parish — including but not limited to teaching young children how to sing, supporting other community events, and participating in music ministry seminars. These activities reflect the Choir's mission to grow and serve.

**Section 4. Digital Presence and Website.**
The Choir recognizes the value of a digital presence for growth, outreach, and accessibility. The Choir may maintain:
- An **official social media account** for documentation, announcements, and community engagement
- An **official website** for membership information, choir history, event updates, and sponsorship inquiries

The website and social media are managed by the Documentation & Social Media Committee under the supervision of the External Coordinator. All content must comply with the Data Privacy provisions of Article XIV.

The Choir envisions the website eventually supporting **online membership registration, digital document signing, and sponsor solicitation** — reducing paperwork and improving accessibility for all members and future generations.

## ARTICLE X — MUSIC AND REPERTOIRE

**Section 1.** The Choir Director / Music Lead shall coordinate song selection with the parish liturgical guidelines and present a song list to members in advance of each mass when possible.

**Section 2.** A **Master Repertoire List** shall be maintained by the Secretary and Music Lead, categorized as:
- *Mastered* — songs the choir can sing confidently without extensive rehearsal
- *Learning* — songs currently being practiced
- *Retired* — songs no longer in rotation but part of choir history

**Section 3.** Song selection disputes shall be resolved by a discussion between the Music Lead and Choir Adviser. If unresolved, the President casts the deciding vote.

**Section 4.** Members are encouraged to suggest songs through the Music Lead. Suggestions shall be acknowledged and considered, not ignored.

## ARTICLE XI — DISCIPLINE AND DUE PROCESS

**Section 1.** San Vicente Choir does not believe in punitive discipline for attendance. Members who cannot attend are not fined or penalized — they are reclassified and supported.

**Section 2.** However, behavior that causes harm to the community — harassment, public shaming, theft of choir funds, repeated disrespect toward members or officers — shall be addressed through the following process:

1. Private conversation between the officer and the member
2. If unresolved, a formal meeting with at least 3 officers
3. If still unresolved, a vote by all Active Members at a General Assembly
4. Removal requires a **two-thirds (2/3) majority vote** of Active Members present

**Section 3.** No member shall be removed without this process being followed in full.

## ARTICLE XII — MEETINGS

**Section 1. General Assembly.**
The Choir holds two annual assemblies:

**(a) May Anniversary Assembly** — held on the 2nd or 3rd weekend of May, coinciding with the Choir's founding anniversary, to:
- Celebrate the Choir's anniversary
- **Elect incoming officers** by majority vote of Active Members present
- **Conduct oath-taking of newly elected officers** — held at the same assembly or within the last week of May, but no later
- Recognize outgoing officers and long-serving members
- Outgoing officers begin the transition handover to incoming officers

**(b) June General Assembly** — held every June as the **first official assembly led by the newly sworn-in officers**, to:
- Formally open the new choir year under new leadership
- Complete membership renewal — all members sign the Annual Renewal Form
- Allow each member to state their personal goal for the year
- Present plans and priorities for the new choir year
- Present the annual financial report and opening balance from the Treasurer
- Formally welcome new members who joined since the last assembly
- Set the rehearsal schedule and monthly meeting calendar for the semester ahead

**Section 2. Monthly Members Meeting.**
At least **one (1) mandatory general meeting per month** shall be held for all Active Members. Officers may call up to two (2) meetings per month as needed.

- Meetings may be held in person or through an agreed digital platform
- All Active Members are expected to attend
- **Quorum for meetings: 5 Active Members** (officers count toward quorum). This ensures that at minimum the core officer group can conduct business without the meeting being paralyzed by low turnout.
- Decisions require a **simple majority of members present** — not of the total membership
- Meeting agenda must be shared in the group chat **at least 2 days in advance**
- Minutes shall be recorded by the Secretary and shared with all members after the meeting
- Members who cannot attend must notify an officer beforehand and shall be updated on what was discussed

**Section 3. Officer Meetings.**
Officers shall meet separately as needed to manage operations, resolve internal matters, and prepare for general meetings. Officer meetings do not require full member attendance.

**Section 4. Notice.**
All meetings require at least **3 days notice** to all members via the group chat, except in emergency situations.

**Section 5. Decision-Making.**
- Decisions at General Assemblies and monthly meetings require a **simple majority** of Active Members present, except as otherwise specified in this Constitution
- In case of a tie, the President casts the deciding vote

## ARTICLE XIII — PERSONAL ANNUAL GOALS

**Section 1.** At every June General Assembly, each Active Member is invited — not required — to share one personal goal for their time in the Choir for the coming year.

**Section 2.** Goals may be musical (learn a new voice part, gain confidence in solo), personal (attend more consistently, meet new members), or spiritual (deepen my worship through music).

**Section 3.** These goals are personal commitments, not performance metrics. They are not evaluated or graded. They exist so that every member, when they eventually leave the Choir, can look back and say: *"I grew here."*

## ARTICLE XIV — DATA PRIVACY AND MEDIA CONSENT

**Section 1. Purpose.**
San Vicente Choir respects the privacy and dignity of every member. This article governs how the Choir collects, uses, and shares personal information and media content in compliance with the Republic of the Philippines Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

**Section 2. Personal Information Collected.**
The Choir may collect and retain the following information for organizational purposes only:
- Full name, nickname, contact number, and email address
- Voice part and section
- Membership status and attendance records
- Emergency contact (optional but encouraged)

This information shall be stored securely by the Secretary and shall not be shared with any third party without the member's consent.

**Section 3. Media and Photo/Video Consent.**
By signing the Membership Form, each member acknowledges and consents that:

*(a)* Photos, videos, and recordings taken during choir activities — including but not limited to masses, rehearsals, performances, competitions, retreats, birthday celebrations, and other choir events — **may be posted on the choir's official social media accounts and other public platforms** for documentation, promotion, and community purposes.

*(b)* The Choir will make reasonable efforts to ensure that photos and videos posted publicly are appropriate, respectful, and do not compromise the dignity of any member.

*(c)* A member may request that a specific photo or video featuring them be removed from any choir platform by notifying the Secretary or Social Media Coordinator in writing (message is acceptable). The Choir will honor such requests promptly and without question.

*(d)* **For members who are minors (below 18 years of age):** A parent or guardian must co-sign the Membership Form to provide consent for the minor's participation and for any media featuring the minor to be posted publicly. The Choir shall not post identifiable photos or videos of minors without this parental/guardian consent on file.

**Section 4. Internal Communications.**
Content shared within the choir's private group chat — including personal updates, concerns, or discussions — shall remain within the group and shall not be shared, screenshotted, or posted publicly without the consent of all parties involved. Violation of this section is a breach of member trust and shall be addressed under Article XI (Discipline and Due Process).

**Section 5. Protection from External Parties.**
The Choir officers shall not release any member's personal information — including contact details, attendance records, membership status, or personal circumstances — to any external party (including parents of adult members, parish staff, or the public) without the written consent of the member concerned, except where required by law.

For **minor members**, officers may communicate with parents or guardians regarding the minor's participation, welfare, or conduct as necessary for the minor's safety and with appropriate discretion.

**Section 6. Data Retention.**
Membership records shall be retained for the duration of a member's active involvement and for up to three (3) years after their departure, for historical and organizational reference. After this period, personal data may be anonymized or securely disposed of.

**Section 7. Concerns and Complaints.**
Any member who believes their privacy has been violated may raise the concern with the President or any officer. The matter shall be addressed through the process in Article IX. Members also have the right to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC) of the Philippines if they believe their rights under RA 10173 have been violated.

## ARTICLE XV — AMENDMENTS

**Section 1.** This Constitution may be amended at any General Assembly by a **two-thirds (2/3) majority vote** of Active Members present.

**Section 2.** Proposed amendments must be shared with all members at least **7 days** before the assembly.

**Section 3.** All amendments shall be recorded by the Secretary, signed by the President and Vice President, and attached to this Constitution as a permanent record.
