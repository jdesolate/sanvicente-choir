ℹ️ About This Document Hub

This page contains the official documents of San Vicente Choir. Start with the ⭐ Working Rules 2026–2027 — that is what we follow this choir year, as agreed at the General Assembly of July 26, 2026. The Constitution remains a working draft, discussed and ratified article by article at each monthly assembly, and the Handbook is being updated to match. For questions, contact any officer directly.
