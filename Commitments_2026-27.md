# Our Commitments — Term 2026–2027

The full Constitution is our long-term foundation. This page is what we're
actually focusing on together this first year.

## Who we are this year
- Active members — commit to the weekly services; hold voting rights.
- Associate members — serving when life allows; always welcome back.
- Honorary members — long-serving voices, welcome to sing anytime.

## What we're committing to
1. A steady weekly rhythm — show up for the Saturday and Sunday 7PM masses,
   and answer the Thursday poll honestly. A "no" helps us plan just as much
   as a "yes".
2. We serve willingly, never by guilt — no public call-outs or shaming.
   Concerns go privately to an officer first (Code of Conduct, Art. IV & VII).
3. If you go quiet, we'll check on you with care — to understand, not to scold.
4. One shared, free dress code for now — neat top and proper bottoms per the
   attire rules. Real uniforms come later, when funded.
5. We rebuild connection — a short gathering each month, and your voice is
   genuinely wanted. Even associate and honorary members are asked their view.

## What we're NOT rushing this year
We won't fill every officer and section-leader role at once, and we'll keep
the meeting load light. We grow into the full structure as we grow in numbers.

## The one ask for June 20
Come ready to share one personal goal for your year in the choir (Article XIII)
— musical, personal, or spiritual. That's how we start.
