## 1. MEMBERSHIP STATUS

| | **Active** | **Associate (On Leave)** | **Inactive** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ministry regular-members list | ✅ | — | — |
| Active group chat | ✅ | General / Lyrics GC | General / Lyrics GC |
| Voting rights | ✅ | — | — |
| Uniform priority | ✅ | Next batch on return | — |
| Commitment fines apply | ✅ | — | — |
| Major Events | Expected | 5 practices for that event first | — |
| Core masses (Sat & Sun) | Expected | Anytime, always welcome | Always welcome |

**How status is decided:** from the attendance record (June–July 2026, 26 events)
**and** from whether you communicate.

- **50% and above → Active.**
- **Officers, accompanists, and ministry support are Active** regardless of
  percentage — their service is not measured by weekend attendance alone.
- **25%–49% → Associate**, if you declare a reason. You may also commit to Active.
- **No response and no declared reason → Inactive.**

> **Silence is what moves someone down — never busyness.** If you tell us, we can
> work with anything.
>
> *Ang paghilom ang hinungdan sa pag-usab sa status — dili ang pagkabusy.*

**Associate is not quitting.** Inactive members stay in the General GC and are always
welcome back.

**How to become Active — same path for everyone:**

1. **Tell any officer** you want to be Active.
2. **Show up for 4 weeks** — at least **3 of the 4**, services and practices.
3. **Confirmed at the next monthly General Assembly**, by majority of the Active
   Members present.

**You can serve from day one.** You may sing at any Saturday or Sunday mass while
you are being confirmed — what waits is the Active GC, the ministry list, and voting.
**Nobody is ever kept from serving God because of a label.**

*Why the 4 weeks and the vote: our list goes to the parish ministry, so it has to be
accurate — and members who are consistent deserve to know that Active means
something. It is not a test of worth; it is just how long it takes to see a rhythm.*

**Every new member starts as Associate.** Not as a test of worth — so you can learn
our songs, join practices, get the lyrics, and see if this fits your life, with no
pressure and no fines from day one. Then the same 4 weeks and the same confirmation
at the assembly as everyone else.

## 1B. WHY WE USE THESE LABELS

**Active is not a rank or a reward. It means more is asked of you.** You are on the
parish ministry's official list, so the parish is counting on you being there. The
privileges below exist *because of* that duty — not instead of it.

> **Our funds and our uniforms come from the services we sing. So the members who
> show up for those services are the first to benefit from them.**
>
> *Ang atong pondo ug uniform naggikan sa mga misa nga atong giserbisyohan. Busa ang
> mga miyembro nga permi naa, sila ang unang mapahimuslan niini.*

**What Active members receive**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Ministry recognition** | Registered with the parish ministry as a regular member — an official record of your service |
| **Uniform** | First batch, at no cost to you |
| **Voice in decisions** | Voting rights at assemblies; only Active Members may hold officer or committee-head positions |
| **Engagements** | First priority for paid engagements and special events; if a talent fee is shared among performers, priority in slots goes to Active Members |
| **Music** | First consideration for solos and feature parts — you cannot lead a song you were not there to rehearse |
| **Formation** | First priority for choir-funded seminars, workshops, and training when slots are limited |
| **Recognition** | Eligible for our recognition and awards program, and acknowledged at assemblies |
| **Certification** | Full certificate of ministry service for school or work requirements, based on your attendance record |
| **Information** | In the Active GC — you hear plans and changes first |

**What Associate members keep** — no fines, no chasing, no guilt · you may sing at
any Saturday or Sunday mass whenever you are free · full access to lyrics, files,
and practices · your place in this choir, always.

**And Associates are never stuck.** Tell an officer you want to be Active, show up
for 4 weeks, and you are confirmed at the next assembly. You can also earn uniform
priority sooner by helping with solicitation or by self-funding. **And you may sing
at any weekend mass the whole time** — the label waits, your service does not.

**Why the boundaries help you too.** The 5-practices rule before a Major Event is
not a wall — it means that when you stand up at the fiesta, you know the song and
you are not afraid. And being Associate means **nobody is disappointed in you.** You
know exactly where you stand, and we know exactly what to plan around. That is
better for everyone than assuming everyone is available and being let down every
weekend.

*Daghan sa atong mga Associate participative kaayo — dili ni punishment. Ang label
naghatag lang og katin-awan: kinsa ang naa sa ministry list, mao ang gilauman. Walay
pagtag-an, walay paghangyo.*

## 2. DECLARED AVAILABILITY *(new this year)*

On your membership form, write down your **standing unavailability** — work shifts,
class schedule, another choir, gigs, a new baby, health.

- **An absence inside what you declared is automatically excused.**
- Outside choirs and commitments are welcome. The limits: our own Saturday and
  Sunday masses and all **Major Events come first**; conflicts with a Major Event
  are declared **3–4 weeks ahead**; your role's duties still get done; and an
  outside commitment may not cause you to miss a Major Event twice in one term.

*Isulat ang imong regular nga schedule nga dili ka available. Kung mag-absent ka
sulod niana, excused ka dayon — walay multa.*

---

## 3. ATTENDANCE FORM

- One weekly form covers **practices and the weekend masses**.
- **Weekend masses:** answer by the announced deadline — the **Friday 9 PM
  snapshot is the official record**.
- **Practices:** confirm before the practice day.
- **Answering "not attending" costs you nothing.** It is how we plan, and it is
  the responsible thing to do.
- **Practice cancellation:** if fewer than **4 Active Members** have confirmed by
  **12 NN on the practice day**, the practice is cancelled and announced right
  away — so nobody travels to an empty rehearsal.

---

## 4. COMMITMENT FINE — ₱20

**The fine is never for being absent.** It applies only when you:

1. **do not answer the form, or do not inform the group at all**, or
2. **say you will attend and then do not come.**

*Ang multa dili para sa pag-absent. Para kini sa (1) wala pagtubag sa form o wala
gyud nagpahibalo sa grupo, o (2) nagsulti nga motambong apan wala miabot.*

- Applies to **practices and services** alike.
- Logged in the portal ledger and paid to the **Treasurer within 2 weeks**.
- **Waiver:** a fine may be waived **only by the Treasurer or Vice President with
  the President's approval**, and the reason is recorded in the ledger where
  members can see it.
- **Unpaid fines suspend privileges** (uniform priority, party eligibility) —
  **never your membership**, and never a public call-out. Just a quiet standing
  balance you can settle anytime.

**Why we do this:** so that the members who always answer are not the only ones
carrying the planning. Fines exist so communication is fair — **not to raise
money.**

## 5. SINKING FUND — VOLUNTARY

For the **December Christmas party**, collected after Sunday mass and recorded in
the portal.

- **Suggested ₱20 per week · ₱5 for students · any amount is welcome.** Give what
  you can. Nobody is penalized for giving nothing.
- **Earmarked** — it cannot be spent on anything else.
- **Refundable in full on request until the announced cutoff date.** After the
  cutoff the money is committed to the party, and **the contributor list is the
  party list**.
- If you leave the choir before the cutoff, you are **refunded automatically**.
- The party budget is **whatever we collect together**.

*Boluntaryo ang sinking fund. Ang imong hatag, mabalik kung mangayo ka sa wala pa
ang cutoff. Human sa cutoff, para na kini sa Christmas party.*

## 6. BIRTHDAY CELEBRATIONS

**One cake per month for everyone with a birthday that month**, at the monthly
assembly, funded by **amot** — not from choir funds. Two separate cakes cost us
₱520 in two months; doing it monthly saves roughly ₱2,000–₱2,500 a year, which is
most of a uniform.

## 7. OUR GROUP CHATS

| Chat | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Officers GC** | Officer business |
| **SVC Active GC** | The ministry-listed roster: attendance, assignments, funds — anything actionable. |
| **SVC General / Lyrics GC** | Lyrics, song files, general announcements, and onboarding for new members. |

- **Nobody is removed from a group chat as a shortcut.** Associate and Inactive
  members keep the General GC. Removal is possible but only through **due process**,
  never one officer's decision and never over a personal grievance:
  1. Private conversation with an officer — to understand, not to scold
  2. No response in 14 days → officers may reclassify you Inactive *(you keep the
     General GC)*
  3. Removal from our chats only if you ask for it yourself, for serious misconduct
     under Article XI, or for prolonged inactivity with no response — **and only by a
     vote of the Active Members at an assembly**
  4. Anyone removed may return through the normal joining process
- Every choir-wide announcement is **cross-posted to the General GC**.
- We do not discuss members of one chat inside another.
- **Concerns about a person go privately to that person, or to an officer** — never
  to the group chat.

## 8. CHOIR FUNDS — FULL TRANSPARENCY

Three separate funds, all visible in the portal:

| Fund | Purpose | Held by |
|---|---|---|
| **General fund** | Honorarium, event income, fines — transportation, materials, uniforms, choir needs | Treasurer |
| **Sinking fund** | Christmas party only — earmarked | Secretary |
| **Outstanding fines** | Recorded until settled | — |

- The **Treasurer reports the balance at every assembly**, and any member may ask
  to see the records anytime.
- **Monthly verification:** a non-collecting officer confirms the cash matches the
  portal ledger — one officer for the general fund, another for the sinking fund,
  and never a relative of the person holding the money. *This protects the people
  who carry our money, not the other way around.*
- Any expense above **₱300** needs officer majority approval.
- **No funds are ever used for the personal benefit of any officer or member.**

**Uniforms:** funded by the choir and by solicitation — **no cost to members**.
Active members are outfitted first; Associates receive theirs on returning to
Active status in the next batch, or sooner if they self-fund or help with
solicitation.

---

## 9. HOW WE TREAT EACH OTHER

- No public call-outs, shaming, or threats — in the group chat or anywhere.
- Concerns about a person go privately to that person or **privately to an officer first**.
- Group chat content stays in the group chat.
- Before assuming the worst, ask: *"Okay ka ba?"* Absence is not rejection.
- Every member may suggest songs, raise concerns, and be heard.
