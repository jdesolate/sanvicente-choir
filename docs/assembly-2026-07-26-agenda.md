**Announce the three tiers:**

| Tier | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Active** | On the ministry regular-members list · new Active GC · voting rights · uniform priority · subject to commitment fines |
| **Associate (On Leave)** | Declared reason + availability on file · stays in General GC · no fines, no vote · not on ministry list · 5 practices before a Major Event · **may sing at any weekend mass anytime** |
| **Inactive** | No response, no declared reason · off the ministry list · stays in General GC (lyrics + announcements) · always welcome back |

**How anyone becomes Active — one path for everyone:**
> *"Tell any officer you want to be Active. Show up for 4 weeks — at least 3 of the
> 4, services and practices. Then you're confirmed at the next monthly assembly by
> the Active Members. And you can sing at any weekend mass the whole time — the

> of a label."*

**Why Active is worth it — read these out:** on the ministry's official list ·
uniform in the first batch, free · voting rights, and only Active members can be
officers or committee heads · **first priority for paid engagements**, and slot

lead a song you weren't there to rehearse · first priority for choir-funded seminars
· eligible for recognition and awards · **full certificate of ministry service** for
school or work requirements · in the Active GC, so you hear things first.

> *"Our funds and our uniforms come from the masses we serve — so the members who
> serve them benefit first. That's not a punishment for anyone. And nobody is stuck:
> tell an officer, show up 4 weeks, and you're in."*

## 0:30–0:45 · Rules, fines, sinking fund (15 min) — *vote on these*

State the lead line first: **fines exist so communication is fair, not to raise money.**

1. **One weekly form covering practices *and* services** — posted **Thursday
   before 9PM**; the **Friday 9PM snapshot is the official record**. For practices,
   confirm before the practice day.
   *(Open item to settle tonight: a Thursday post is too late to plan a **Tuesday**
   practice. Either move the post earlier in the week, or announce Tuesday practice
   separately. Pick one tonight so the first cancellation isn't a judgment call.)*
2. **Commitment fine — ₱20, for practices and services alike** — for (a) **not
   answering the form or not informing the group at all**, or (b) committing to
   attend and not showing up. Logged in the portal ledger, paid to Mary Love within
   2 weeks.
   **⚠️ THE FINE STARTS NEXT WEEKEND (Aug 1–2), NOT THIS ONE.** Say this out loud:
   *"The rule starts next weekend so everyone has a fair chance to adjust. We'll
   post reminders Thursday and Friday."* **Do not skip this line** — 12 of the 18
   people who answered this weekend's form answered on Saturday itself, including
   me. A Friday deadline applied to this weekend would have fined most of this room
   and its president.
   **Practice quorum cancellation:** fewer than 4 Active Members confirmed by
   **12NN on the practice day** → practice is cancelled and announced immediately,
   so nobody travels to an empty rehearsal. *This is the reason practices are on
   the form.*
3. **Waiver rule (new — this closes a real gap):** a fine may be waived **only by
   the Treasurer/VP with the President's concurrence, and the reason is recorded in
   the ledger where members can see it.** So far ₱40 has been waived and ₱20
   collected — without a written rule that becomes favoritism, and the members
   who always answer are the ones it costs.
4. **Unpaid fines suspend privileges** (uniform priority, party eligibility) —
   never membership, never a public call-out. A quiet standing balance.
5. **Sinking fund — voluntary and tracked.** Suggested ₱20/week, ₱5 for students,
   any amount welcome. Collected by Gezd after Sunday mass, posted in the portal.
6. **Refund cutoff — announce the date today (recommend Nov 15).** Refundable in
   full on request until the cutoff; committed to the party after. Anyone who
   leaves the choir before the cutoff is refunded automatically. After the
   cutoff, **the contributor list is the party list.**
7. **Christmas party budget is whatever we collect** — the fund is earmarked and
   cannot be spent on anything else.
8. **Birthday cakes** — one cake per month for all that month's birthdays, at the
   assembly, funded by **amot**, not choir funds. Say the number: *"two cakes cost
   us ₱520 in two months; batching saves roughly ₱2,000–2,500 a year — most of a
   uniform."*
9. **Becoming Active — the same 4 weeks for everyone.** New members, returnees, and
   Associates moving up all follow one path: tell an officer → **4 weeks, at least 3
   of the 4** (practices and services) → **confirmed at the next monthly assembly by
   majority of the Active Members present**. They sing at weekend masses the whole
   time; what waits is the Active GC, the ministry list, and voting. *Say why: our
   list goes to the parish ministry, so it has to be accurate — and one appearance
   isn't a rhythm.*

**Take the vote** (simple majority of those present, Art. XII §5) and have Gezd
