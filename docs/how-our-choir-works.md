## 1B. WHY WE HAVE ALL OF THIS

You may look at this page, the rules, and the Constitution and think: *why so
complicated for a small parish choir?* Here is the honest answer.

**Because our lives are all different.** Some of us work. Some work two jobs. Some
are students with exams and school performances. Some are new parents. Some are
reviewing for board exams. Some sing in other choirs. Some live far from Duljo. None
of that is a problem — but it means **we cannot run on assumptions.**

When expectations are unwritten, this is what happens: we guess what others can give,
the guesses are wrong, we get disappointed, disappointment turns into resentment,
resentment turns into gossip — and people leave quietly without ever saying why.
**We have seen it before. That is the confusion we are ending.**

So everything here exists for three reasons:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Stability** | The members who carry the weekly load deserve to know that showing up counts for something, and that they are not the only ones being counted on |
| **Participation** | The members who cannot be core deserve a real place in this choir — not a guilty spot at the edge of it |
| **Clarity** | A brand-new member should be able to read one page, understand how we work, and take part meaningfully — without embarrassment and without being judged |

**And three things this is designed to prevent:**

- **Guilt.** Nobody should feel bad for having a life. Tell us honestly and there is
  no penalty and no lecture.
- **Targeting.** No member can be pushed out because one person is frustrated with
  them. That is why removal needs due process and a vote — it protects you, and it
  protects the officer who would otherwise be blamed.
- **Conflict becoming personal.** Disagreements are handled by process, not by
  personality. Concerns about a person: go to
  that person or to an officer — never to the group chat.

> **The rules are not here to control you. They are here so nobody has to be chased,
> nobody has to be judged, and nobody can be pushed out unfairly.**
>
> *Kining mga patakaran dili para pugngan ka. Naa ni para walay gukdon, walay
> hukman, ug walay mapapahawa nga walay husto nga proseso.*

**And in practice, it is simple.** Whatever else is written here, your week is only
this:

```
1. Answer the form.
2. Come when you can.
3. Tell us when you can't.
```

Everything else in this document exists so that those three things are enough.

## 2. HOW MEMBERSHIP WORKS

```
Interested  →  Register in the portal  →  Read the rules  →  Sign the form
     →  Audition (welcome tradition, not a test)  →  Voice section assigned
     →  ASSOCIATE for 4 weeks  →  approved by active members  →  ACTIVE
```

| Status | Who | On ministry list | Fines | Vote |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Active** | The core — expected at services and practices | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Associate** | Participative, or on leave with a declared reason | — | — | — |
| **Inactive** | No presence and no declared reason | — | — | — |
| **Honorary** | Long-serving voices, welcome to sing anytime | — | — | — |

- **Active means more is asked of you**, not that you rank higher. The privileges
  exist because of the duty.
- **Every new member starts as Associate** — no fines, no pressure, full access to
  lyrics and practices, while you see if this fits your life.
- **Silence is what changes your status — never busyness.** Tell us and we can work
  with almost anything.

**How to become Active** *(new members, returning Associates, everyone — same path)*

```
1. Tell any officer you want to be Active
2. Show up for 4 weeks — at least 3 weeks of the 4, services and practices
3. Confirmed at the next monthly General Assembly, by majority of Active Members present
```

**You can serve from day one.** You may sing at any Saturday or Sunday mass while
you're being confirmed — what waits is the Active GC, the ministry list, and voting.
Nobody is ever kept from serving God because of a label.

Why the 4 weeks and the vote: our list goes to the parish ministry, so it has to be
accurate — and consistent members deserve to know that Active means something. It is
not a test of worth. It is just how long it takes to see a rhythm.

## 3. THE WEEKLY FLOW

```
THU  Attendance form posted (practices + weekend masses)
FRI  9PM — snapshot taken; this is the official record
SAT  3–5PM optional gathering  ·  7PM Mass, SVF Chapel
SUN  7PM Mass, San Nicolas  ·  sinking fund collected after
TUE  Practice (7:30 call · 7:45 warm-up · 8:00 sharp)
THU  Practice
```

- **Answering "not attending" costs you nothing.** It is how we plan.
- **A practice is cancelled** if fewer than 4 Active Members have confirmed by 12NN
  that day — so nobody travels to an empty rehearsal.
- **A mass needs 4 members with at least 1 per voice part.** Below that, an officer
  decides whether to proceed or notify the parish.
- **End of every month: General Assembly** — funds report, 2–3 Constitution articles
  discussed, one cake for that month's birthdays.

## 4. HOW WE COMMUNICATE

| Where | For what |
|---|---|
| **Officers GC** | Officer business; conflicts stay here or go to the President |
| **Active GC** | The ministry-listed roster: attendance, assignments, funds, coordination |
| **General / Lyrics GC** | Lyrics, song files, announcements, onboarding — Associates and Inactive members stay here permanently |
| **Choir Portal** | The permanent home for songs, lyrics, attendance, funds, and documents. Nothing gets buried here. |
| **Facebook Page** | Our public face — announcements, media, sponsor inquiries |

**Nobody is removed from a group chat as a shortcut.** Removal is possible, but only
through due process — never one officer's decision, and never because of a personal
grievance:

```
1. Private conversation with an officer — to understand, not to scold
2. No response in 14 days → officers meet and may reclassify you Inactive
   (you still keep the General / Lyrics GC)
3. Removal from the choir's chats only if: you ask for it yourself · serious
   misconduct under Article XI · or prolonged inactivity with no response —
   and only by a vote of the Active Members at an assembly
4. Anyone removed may return through the normal joining process
```

This is deliberately slow. It protects every member from being pushed out over
someone's frustration, and it protects officers from being blamed for a decision the
choir made together.

Every choir-wide announcement is cross-posted to the General GC. **Concerns about a
person go privately to that person, or to an officer — never to the group chat.**

## 5. HOW MONEY WORKS

| Fund | Where it comes from | What it is for | Held by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **General fund** | Parish honorarium, event income, fines | Transportation, uniforms, materials, choir needs | Treasurer |
| **Sinking fund** | Voluntary weekly contributions | **Christmas party only** — earmarked | Secretary |
| **Fines** | ₱20 commitment fines | Into the general fund | Treasurer |

**Rules everyone should know**

- **No membership dues.** The sinking fund is voluntary — suggested ₱20/week, ₱5 for
  students, any amount welcome, and **refundable until the announced cutoff**.
- **The ₱20 fine is never for being absent.** Only for **not answering the form or
  not informing the group**, or for **saying you will come and not coming**. Unpaid
  fines suspend privileges — never your membership.
- **A fine may only be waived** by the Treasurer or VP with the President's approval,
  and the reason is recorded where members can see it.
- **Anything above ₱300** needs officer majority approval.
- **Every month, a non-collecting officer verifies** that the cash matches the portal
  ledger — and never a relative of the person holding the money.
- **Any member may ask to see the records at any time.**
- Care is never gated by status — community assistance, excuse and leave letters,
  condolence singing, and birthday greetings are for **every** member.

## 6. WHO DOES WHAT

**Officers** — President · Vice President & Internal Coordinator · Choir Adviser ·
Music Director & Liturgical Lead · Secretary · Treasurer · External Coordinator

**Section leaders** — Soprano, Alto, Tenor, Bass. They help their section prepare and
check on section mates who go quiet.

**Committees** — Music · Membership · Logistics & Property · Documentation & Social
Media · External & Solicitation

**Every member is asked to carry one task.** The full list of about ninety recurring
jobs is written down and open — from posting lyrics to setting up the sound system to
being the point person for one event. Nothing in this choir should depend on one
person.

## 7. HOW DECISIONS ARE MADE

| Decision | How |
|---|---|
| Day-to-day | Officers decide; the President has the final call |
| Rules, funds, plans | Simple majority of Active Members present at an assembly |
| Meeting quorum | **5 Active Members**, officers included |
| Constitution amendments | Two-thirds of Active Members present, shared 7 days ahead |
| Removing a member for misconduct | Full due process — private talk, then 3 officers, then a two-thirds assembly vote |
| Musical disputes | Music Director with the Adviser; President breaks a tie |
| Officer conflicts | Stay with officers or come to the President — never to the members |

## 10. WHAT WE ARE BUILDING TOWARD

A choir that **does not depend on any one person.** Systems instead of pure effort,
so that when someone is tired, busy, or away, the ministry continues. Clear
expectations so nobody has to be chased, and nobody has to feel guilty. And a
Constitution written *with* the members, not handed down to them.

> *We serve willingly — not by force, not by guilt, but by love for God and for each
> other.*
