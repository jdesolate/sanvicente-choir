# Our Commitments — Term 2026–2027

The full Constitution is our long-term foundation. This page is the spirit of what
we're building together this year. For the exact rules — status, attendance, fines,
funds — see the Working Rules 2026–2027.

## Who we are this year
- **Active members** — on the ministry's regular-members list. Commit to the weekly
  services and the Tuesday & Thursday practices, and answer the attendance form
  every week. Hold voting rights and uniform priority.
- **Associate members (on leave)** — serving when life allows, with a declared
  reason on file: work, studies, board review, health, a new child, another
  ministry. No fines. Can sing at Core Masses anytime; for a Major Event, attend 5
  practices for it first. To become Active: tell an officer, show up for 4 weeks,
  and be confirmed at the next monthly assembly — while singing freely the whole
  time.
- **Inactive members** — no response and no declared status. Off the ministry list,
  but never out of the choir: they keep the General/Lyrics group chat and are
  always welcome back.
- **Honorary members** — long-serving voices, welcome to sing anytime.

> Silence is what changes someone's status — never busyness.

## What we're committing to
1. A steady weekly rhythm — the Saturday and Sunday 7PM masses, and our standing
   Tuesday & Thursday practice. Answer the form every week; a "no" helps us plan
   just as much as a "yes", and it costs nothing.
2. We keep our word to each other. The ₱20 commitment fine is not a punishment for
   absence — only for not answering the form, or committing and not coming. It
   exists so the members who always communicate aren't carrying the planning alone.
3. We fund our year together, without pressure. The sinking fund is voluntary and
   refundable until the cutoff; the Christmas party is whatever we build together.
   Every peso is recorded in the portal and verified monthly by an officer who does
   not hold the money.
4. Saturdays are ours, 3–5PM — practice, recreation, content, or bonding.
5. We serve willingly, never by guilt — no public call-outs or shaming. Concerns go
   privately to an officer first (Code of Conduct, Art. IV & VII).
6. If you go quiet, we'll check on you with care — to understand, not to scold.
7. **Nobody is removed from a group chat as a shortcut.** Active members coordinate
   in the Active GC; everyone else keeps the General/Lyrics GC, and every choir-wide
   announcement is posted there too. Removal is possible but follows due process and
   needs a vote of the Active Members — never one person's frustration.
8. Uniforms this year, at no cost to members — funded by choir funds and
   solicitation. Active members first; Associates on return to Active, or sooner by
   self-funding or helping with solicitation.
9. One birthday cake per month at the end-of-month assembly, funded by amot — so
   choir funds go to uniforms and real needs.
10. Our media committee documents what we do, because this year is worth
    remembering.
11. Everyone registers in the portal with complete information, including declared
    availability.
12. A recognition program is being planned — consistent, dedicated members
    acknowledged.

## What we're still building
Section leaders and some committee roles are filled as we grow. We're training more
accompanists so the choir never depends on one person being available.

**Our Constitution is a draft, and that's on purpose.** We discuss two or three
articles at every monthly assembly until the whole choir has read it, questioned
it, and agreed to it. It is meant to be ours — not handed down.

## The one ask at every assembly
Come ready to share one personal goal for your year in the choir (Article XIII) —
musical, personal, or spiritual. Not a grade. Just so that one day you can look
back and say: *"I grew here."*
