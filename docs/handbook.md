## 👋 WELCOME! / WELCOME KA DINHI!

*English:*
Welcome to San Vicente Choir — a community that has been singing for God for over 54 years. Whether you are new or returning, we are so glad you are here. This handbook is your guide to everything you need to know as a member. Please read it fully and ask any officer if you have questions. We are here for you. 💛

*Bisaya:*
Welcome sa San Vicente Choir — usa ka komunidad nga nag-awit alang sa Ginoo sulod sa labaw sa 54 ka tuig. Bag-o ka man o nagbalik, nalipay kaayo mi nga naa ka dinhi. Kining handbook imong giya sa tanan nga kinahanglan nimong mahibal-an isip miyembro. Palihug basaha kini bug-os ug pangutana ang bisan unsang opisyal kung adunay pangutana. Naa mi para kanimo. 💛

---

## 🙏 WHY WE EXIST / NGANONG NANHA TA DINHI

*English:* We sing not because we are forced to. We sing because we choose to offer our voices to God. Every voice matters — whether you've been here 30 years or 30 days. You are here because you want to be, and that is enough.

*Bisaya:* Nag-awit ta dili kay gipugos. Nag-awit ta kay gusto nato ihalad ang atong tingog sa Ginoo. Ang matag tingog importante — bisan 30 ka tuig o 30 ka adlaw ka pa lang dinhi. Naa ka dinhi kay gusto nimo — ug igo na kana.

---

## 🎙️ WHO CAN JOIN / KINSA ANG PWEDE MOSALI

*English:*

**Singing Members:** At least Grade 7 (~13 years old). No maximum age. No experience needed — just willingness. Must register and complete all fields in the membership form.

**Ministry Support Members** *(Instrumentalists)*: No age restriction. Must be approved by the Music Director. Same conduct and attire standards apply.

**For members below 18:** A parent or guardian must co-sign the Membership Form before joining any activity.

*You do not need to be perfect. You just need to want to be here.* 💛

---

## 📝 HOW TO JOIN / UNSAON SA PAGSALI

*English:*
1. 🙋 Tell any officer you're interested
2. 📋 **Register on the choir portal** — complete all fields. No field may be left blank.
3. 📖 Read this Handbook and the Constitution fully
4. ✍️ Sign the Membership Form (parent/guardian co-signs if below 18)
5. 🎤 **Audition** — sing one song of your choice. Not a pass/fail test — a welcome tradition to find your voice section
6. 🎵 Get your section assignment from the Music Director
7. 👋 Be introduced to the full choir!

*Everyone who auditions in good faith is welcomed.* 🎶

*Bisaya:*
1. 🙋 Sultihi ang bisan unsang opisyal nga gusto kang mosali
2. 📋 **Mag-register sa choir portal** — pun-a ang tanan nga fields. Walay field nga mahimong blanko.
3. 📖 Basaha kining Handbook ug ang Saligang Batas bug-os
4. ✍️ Pirmahan ang Membership Form (ginikanan/guardian mag-co-sign kung menor de edad)
5. 🎤 **Mag-audition** — usa ka awit sa imong pagpili. Dili pass/fail — matahom nga tradisyon para mahibal-an ang imong voice section
6. 🎵 Dawata ang imong section assignment gikan sa Music Director
7. 👋 Opisyal nga ipaila sa tibuok koro!

## 🗓️ SATURDAY GATHERING / SABADO NGA TIGOM

*English:*
Every Saturday, **3:00 PM – 5:00 PM**, before the mass — the choir gathers for one of the following:

- 🎵 Practice / Rehearsal
- 🎬 Content creation (photos, videos, social media)
- 🏃 Recreational activities / sports
- 📚 Seminar or workshop
- 🤝 Community service
- 🎉 Bonding and fellowship

Not mandatory — but this is when the group is built outside of the mass. Come when you can. 💛

*Bisaya:*
Matag Sabado, **3:00 PM – 5:00 PM**, bago ang misa — nagtigom ang koro para sa usa niining mga aktibidad:

- 🎵 Praktis / Ensayo
- 🎬 Content creation (mga litrato, video, social media)
- 🏃 Recreational / sports
- 📚 Seminar o workshop
- 🤝 Community service
- 🎉 Bonding ug fellowship

Dili mandatory — apan mao kini ang panahon kung asa nagtukod ang grupo gawas sa misa. Anhi kung makakaya. 💛

- 📱 Phones for music sheets only — no social media during mass
- 👀 Be attentive — watch the Music Director for cues
- 🎵 Sing with intention every song

## 🕊️ ASSUME POSITIVE INTENTION / PAG-ALAM OG POSITIBONG KINAIYA

We serve God alongside people with different schedules, personalities, and circumstances. Before reacting to a missed mass, a late reply, or a comment that felt wrong — pause and ask: *"Is there a good reason I don't know yet?"*

1. 🙋 **Before assuming the worst — ask first.** A simple *"Okay ka ba?"* goes further than a complaint.
2. 📱 **Tone is hard to read in text.** If a message seems rude or cold, assume it was written in a hurry — not in anger.
3. 🪑 **Absence is not rejection.** When someone doesn't show up or doesn't reply, they likely have a reason. Check on them — don't judge them.
4. 🤔 **Disagreement is not a personal attack.** When someone questions a plan, they probably care about the group — not targeting you.
5. 🗣️ **Speak to the person, not about them.** Bring concerns to the person directly or to an officer — not the group chat or side conversations.
6. 🌿 **Give grace.** Everyone has hard days at school, work, or home. What looks like bad attitude is sometimes just exhaustion.

*Bisaya: Nagsilbi ta sa Diyos kauban ang mga tawo nga nagkalain-laing iskedyul ug sirkumstansya. Bago moreaksyon — ihunong ug pangutana: "Aduna bay maayong rason nga wala pa nako mahibal-i?" Hatagi ang matag tawo og dignidad nga masabtan una bago hukman. Mao kana kung unsaon pagkahimo sa usa ka koro nga komunidad.* 💛

## 🗓️ ATTENDANCE / PRESENSYA

We do not punish absence. We ask only that you communicate. 💬

**Submit the Google Form before every service.** The Secretary checks the portal before each mass.

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| 🏥 Work, exam, medical, family emergency | No explanation needed — submit the form |
| ✈️ Out of town, school org, personal | Submit the form and message any officer before mass |
| 🔇 No form submitted, no notice | The only thing we ask you to avoid |

After 3 consecutive unexcused absences, an officer checks on you — not to scold, but to make sure you're okay. 🫂

## 🌿 MEMBER STATUS / STATUS SA MIYEMBRO

- 🟢 **Active Member** — regular participant, holds voting rights
- 🟡 **Associate / On Leave** — busy right now, still family. Can sing at **Core Masses** (Sat/Sun) anytime. To join a **Major or Special Event**, must attend at least **5 practice sessions** for that event. Return to Active status anytime.
- 🏅 **Honorary Member** — long-serving member, always welcome to sing

*Going on leave is not quitting. Returning is always possible.* 💛

## 🚪 LEAVING THE CHOIR

Tell any officer personally. You will receive a proper farewell. You will **never** be removed from the group chat without your knowledge and consent. 🎶

## 💰 THE CHOIR'S MONEY / ANG PONDO SA KORO

San Vicente Choir is voluntary. No membership dues. Funds — from honoraria, talent fees, donations, or sponsorships — belong to all members and are used for group needs: transportation, uniforms, events, equipment, and when needed, to help a member in difficult times. 🤝

**This year's priority:** Choir uniforms, funded through sponsorship — no cost to members.

Financial report shared at every General Assembly. Any member may ask to see records anytime. Full transparency. 📊

---

## 🔒 YOUR PRIVACY / ANG IMONG PRIVACY

Your personal info is kept by the Secretary for choir records only — never shared without your consent.

Photos/videos from choir activities may be posted on official platforms by our media officers (Hany and Fatima, coordinated by Gezd). To have any photo of you removed, just message the Secretary — no questions asked. 🙏

Group chat content stays in the group chat. Screenshots shared publicly without consent are a violation of trust.

**Members below 18:** Parent/guardian must co-sign your form. Identifiable photos of minors will not be posted without parental consent.

Rights under RA 10173 — you may file a complaint with the **National Privacy Commission (NPC)** if needed.
