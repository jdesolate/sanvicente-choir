# Share the Load — Complete Task List & Sign-Up
## San Vicente Choir · Choir Year 2026–2027

*This is every recurring job in our choir, written down so nothing is invisible and
nothing depends on one person. **Every member is asked to take one.** Not a big
thing — one thing.*

**Legend:** 📱 doable from your phone · 💻 needs a laptop or PC · 🙋 needs you
physically present · Frequency: W weekly · M monthly · E per event · Y yearly

## What the President keeps

Representing the choir to the parish and ministry · final decisions and conflict
mediation · portal development.

## Everything else is available

**If you can carry one thing, write your name.** If you take something and it turns
out not to fit you, say so — we will move it, no hard feelings. And if you cannot
take anything right now, that is genuinely fine. Say that too, and we won't ask
again this month.

> *Five people doing everything is what we have now. Twenty people doing one small
> thing each is a choir that lasts.*
