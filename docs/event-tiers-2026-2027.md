# What Counts As What — Event Tiers & Practices
## San Vicente Choir · Choir Year 2026–2027

*So that every member knows exactly what is expected, and when.*

---

## The four kinds of choir activity

| | What it is | Expected of | Answer the form? | ₱20 fine applies? | Counts toward your Active/Associate status? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 🟡 **CORE** | Our two weekly masses | All Active Members | **Yes, every week** | **Yes** | **Yes** |
| 🔴 **MAJOR** | Big parish liturgies & ministry services | All Active Members, unless excused | **Yes** | **Yes** | **Yes** |
| 🟣 **SPECIAL** | Gigs, competitions, engagements, seminars | Voluntary | Yes, so we can plan | **No** | Yes, as a bonus — never against you |
| 🟢 **PRACTICE** | Rehearsals & training | All Active Members | **Yes, every week** | **Yes** | **Yes** |

**Two things to remember:**

1. **The ₱20 fine is never about being absent.** It is only for (a) not answering
   the form, or (b) saying you will come and then not coming. Answering "not
   attending" costs you nothing — it is how we plan.
2. **Practices are on the form too, with the same rules.** We need to know who is
   coming so we can cancel a practice that will not reach quorum — nobody should
   travel to an empty rehearsal.
3. **Practices count toward your Active/Associate status.** Our attendance record
   covers services *and* practices together.

| **Saturday 7:00 PM** | Mass — San Vicente Ferrer Chapel, Duljo Barangay Hall |
| **Sunday 7:00 PM** | Mass — Archdiocesan Shrine of San Nicolas de Tolentino Parish |

- This is the heart of the ministry. Everything else is built around it.
- **Minimum to proceed with a mass: 4 members, at least 1 per voice part.** If we
  fall short, the President or an officer decides whether to proceed or to notify
  the parish coordinator — never a single member's burden.
- **Associate members may sing at Core masses anytime** they are available and
  willing. No practice requirement.

## 🟣 SPECIAL — voluntary, always announced in advance

Never fined, never held against you. Officers vote before committing the choir.

- Singing engagements and talent-fee gigs — weddings, thanksgiving masses,
  corporate or community events *(e.g. Tupaba Thanksgiving Mass, July 6)*
- Wake masses, funeral masses, and *responso* for families
- Competitions and concerts
- Seminars, workshops, and formation activities
- Christmas Caroling (early December) and the Choir Christmas Party
- Feast days and parish services outside the Major list
- 🟠 **Saturday Gathering, 3:00–5:00 PM** — rotating: recreation, content
  creation, seminar, community service, practice, or fellowship. Optional but
  encouraged; announced at least 3 days ahead.

## 🟢 PRACTICES — three kinds

**1. Standing weekly training — Tuesday & Thursday**
Our regular rehearsal with the Music Director. This is where the choir is actually
built. *(While Vince is away, training runs to guide recordings with our
accompanist trainees — Merv, Amor, Paolo.)*

**2. Monthly rehearsal — minimum one per month, all year**
Required by our own rules even in quiet months.

**3. Intensified practice before Major Events — 1 to 3 sessions per week**
Before the September fiestas, the Christmas season, and Holy Week.

**Rehearsal time standard**

| Time | |
|---|---|
| 7:30 PM | Call time — arrive |
| 7:45 PM | Warm-up |
| 8:00 PM | Rehearsal starts **sharp**, once 4 Active Members are present |

- A song plan is shared **before** the rehearsal day.
- **Answer the form for practices too**, same ₱20 rule as services.
- **Quorum cancellation:** if fewer than **4 Active Members** have confirmed by
  **12 NN on the practice day**, the practice is cancelled and announced right
  away — so nobody travels to an empty rehearsal. It is rescheduled at the next
  available date.
- **Personal practice** — listening to your part at home — is not a rehearsal, but
  it is what makes rehearsals worth attending.
- Also cancelled for heavy rain or typhoon signal, emergencies affecting most
  members, or a parish suspension — announced as early as possible.

---

## 😴 REST PERIODS — no practices, mass obligations continue

After every Major season, minimum **one week** with no rehearsals or extra
activities: **Dec 26–31** after Christmas, and **Mar 29 – early April** after Holy
Week. Consistent members deserve recovery.
